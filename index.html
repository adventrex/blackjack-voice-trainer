How to play / voice commands

Set it up before you drive, tap Start, then put the phone down. Keep Safari open on screen (your car's map can stay on CarPlay).

Every card is read aloud. Keep the Hi-Lo running count in your head: 2–6 = +1, 7–9 = 0, 10/face/Ace = −1. The dealer's face-down card counts when it's flipped.

On your turn say hit, stand, double or split. After each hand you'll hear "Running count?" — say a number like "plus three", "minus two" or "zero".

Betting: before each hand you'll hear "Your bet?" Say units ("two") or dollars ("twenty dollars"). Work out the true count = running count ÷ decks left, then bet: true count +1 or less → 1 unit, +2 → 2, +3 → 4, +4 → 6, +5 or more → 8. Each +1 of true count is worth about +0.5% to you; the house is ahead until about +1. Bet wrong and it tells you the right bet and why.

Hit 80% over your last 20 count checks and a player is added automatically (up to 7).

Anytime you're asked something you can also say: repeat, score, pause (then resume), add a player, remove a player, slower, faster.

Rules: 6 decks, dealer stands on soft 17, double after split, one split per hand, reshuffle at 75%. If you go against basic strategy, it explains why. If you miss the count, it walks through that hand's low and high cards.
